04 — How I Work

A process built for
clean handoffs

Every project moves through the same checkpoints, so nothing gets lost between the brief and the final file.

01

Discover

Understand the brief, the audience and what the project actually needs to achieve before opening any design tool.

02

Concept

Explore directions fast — sketches, moodboards and rough layouts to find the idea worth building on.

03

Design

Build the chosen direction out in full — typography, colour, layout and detail, refined in Illustrator, Photoshop or Figma.

04

Refine

Review against the brief, tighten the details and gather feedback until the work is genuinely ready.

05

Deliver

Hand off production-ready files — print-ready, dev-ready, or platform-ready, with QA checked before it ships.

05 — About

The goal stays the same across all of it: design that actually connects with the person on the other end.
